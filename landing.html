Features

Everything you need to manage your org

From real-time org charts to advanced workforce analytics, OrgViz Pro gives you complete visibility into your organization.

Dynamic Org Charts

Interactive, always up-to-date org charts that sync with your HRIS. Drag-and-drop editing with full version history.

Employee Directory

Powerful search across your entire organization. Find anyone by name, title, skills, or department in seconds.

Workforce Analytics

Deep insights into headcount, turnover, tenure, and organizational health. Track trends over time with AI-powered recommendations.

Scenario Planning

Model organizational changes before you make them. Compare scenarios and understand budget impact instantly.

Real-time Sync

Connects with Workday, BambooHR, Rippling, and 50+ HR systems. Always accurate, always current.

Enterprise Security

SOC 2 Type II certified. SSO, SCIM, and granular role-based access controls. Your data stays yours.
